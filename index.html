Every league you play in. One live scoreboard.

AllRosters puts all of your Sleeper matchups on one screen, with live scores, the odds you win each one, and the points your bench is costing you. No more flipping between leagues on a Sunday.

Getting started

Set up in under a minute

  1. Open AllRosters

    It runs in any browser on your phone or computer. Nothing to install, though you can add it to your home screen.

  2. Enter your Sleeper username

    Type the username from your Sleeper profile. AllRosters finds every NFL league you're in this season and remembers you in that browser.

  3. Leave it open on game day

    Scores refresh every 45 seconds while games are live. Step back through past weeks with the arrows in the header.

The math

How AllRosters calculates win probability and bench points

Win probability starts from the points already on the board. For each starter still to play, AllRosters takes their Sleeper projection for the week (PPR, half-PPR or standard, whichever your league uses) and scales it by how much of their game is left. That gives a projected final score for you and for your opponent.

Each remaining starter adds uncertainty, and the less of their game is left, the less they can swing it. Those are combined into one spread, and your win probability is the chance, on a normal curve, that your projected margin finishes above zero. Starters listed Out, Doubtful, on IR or suspended count for nothing.

Bench points compare what you scored with the best legal lineup you could have set from your whole roster, filling the most restrictive slots first so flex eligibility is respected.

Data source
Sleeper's public API for rosters, matchups, projections and stats, and Sleeper's live NFL scores for game clocks.
Refresh rate
Every 45 seconds by default while the current week is live. You can pick 30 seconds, 90 seconds or 5 minutes.
Scoring formats
Whatever your league uses. Point breakdowns are priced with each league's own settings.
Lineup slots
QB, RB, WR, TE, K, DEF, FLEX, Superflex, WR/TE and WR/RB flex, and IDP slots.
Privacy
Your username and preferences are saved only in your browser. There are no accounts and nothing is stored on a server.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is AllRosters free?

Yes. There is no account, no subscription and no ads. You enter your Sleeper username and your leagues load.

Do I have to log in to Sleeper or share my password?

No. AllRosters only needs your public Sleeper username. It reads the same public data anyone can see about a Sleeper league, and never asks for a password.

Will it remember my leagues next time?

Yes. Your username is saved in the browser you used, so the scoreboard opens straight to your leagues. Settings also gives you a personal link that brings them back on any other phone or computer, or if your browser's data gets cleared.

Can AllRosters change my lineup or make waiver claims?

No. Sleeper's public API is read-only, so AllRosters can only show your leagues. Lineup changes, trades and waivers still happen in the Sleeper app.

Does it work with ESPN, Yahoo or NFL.com leagues?

Not today. AllRosters works with Sleeper NFL leagues only.

How is win probability calculated?

For each starter still to play, AllRosters takes their projection and scales it by the share of their game left. Adding that to the points already scored gives a projected final for both teams. The uncertainty of each remaining starter is combined, and the chance your projected margin stays above zero on a normal curve is your win probability. Injured and inactive players count for nothing.

What does "left on the bench" mean?

It is the gap between what your lineup scored and the highest-scoring legal lineup you could have set from everyone on your roster that week, including flex eligibility. AllRosters also names the swaps that would have closed the gap.

How often do scores update?

Every 45 seconds by default. You can choose 30 seconds, 90 seconds or 5 minutes in settings, and refresh by hand at any time.

Is AllRosters made by Sleeper?

No. AllRosters is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Sleeper.
